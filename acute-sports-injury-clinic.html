What we don't manage
  • Fractures requiring surgical management
  • Dislocation reductions requiring IV pain relief
  • Severe trauma or multi-system injury
  • High acuity presentations (possible spinal, pelvic, internal injury)
  • Very unwell or drowsy patients
  • Non-sporting injuries (falls, MVAs)
  • Chronic injuries older than 3–4 weeks
If you're unsure, call the clinic before presenting. If you have a suspected serious injury — go straight to the emergency department.

How it works

Simple. Fast. On a Sunday.

Hybrid model — book in advance or walk in. Max 1-hour wait for walk-in patients.

1
Book or walk in
Reserve a 20-min slot online (recommended), or arrive as a walk-in. 1 walk-in spot held per hour — max wait 1 hour.
2
Triage on arrival
Brief triage — sport, club, insurance details, nature of injury. Helps us prepare and process your claim correctly.
3
Assessment & treatment
Full injury assessment by Chris Tandy (Physiotherapist) or Jordan Schorel (Osteopath). Diagnosis, treatment, strapping, bracing as needed.
4
Leave with a plan
Clear diagnosis. Return-to-play timeline. Referrals and letters if needed. You know exactly what to do next — before you leave.
