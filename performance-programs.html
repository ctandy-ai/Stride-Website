Performance testing explained

Why testing
changes the plan

A coach can see a lot just by watching you train: your technique, whether you're holding position under load, whether you're getting stronger on the bar. What a coach can't see is how fast you produce force, how well you use elastic energy, or whether one side is quietly doing less than the other. Those things need measuring.

That matters because they're usually what's holding an athlete back. You can train hard for six weeks on what's visible and never touch the thing that's actually capping you.

Testing every six to eight weeks is how we re-anchor the plan objectively. It's built into Athlete Performance. S&C members access it at $195 per round — a 30% discount off the standard rate.
