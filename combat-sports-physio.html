Common questions
Can I keep training while injured?

Almost always. We map what's off and what's not — drilling when you can't spar, upper body when the knee is out, conditioning while the shoulder heals.

Do you actually understand BJJ / grappling?

Tom has a background in combat training. Every program accounts for guard retention, takedown demands, grading dates, and comp timelines.

Can I see Tom online if I'm not in Melbourne?

Yes. Tom takes online consults Australia-wide through Combat Sports Consulting — diagnosis, rehab plan, weekly progressions and direct support.

What's the difference between Stride and Combat Sports Consulting?

Tom works across both. Stride is the full in-person sports physio clinic. Combat Sports Consulting is Tom's specialist practice focused exclusively on combat athletes, including online consults.

How do I know when I'm ready to spar again?

Objective testing — not a calendar date or gut feel. The Combat Ready System uses criteria-based clearance gates at every stage of return.

Do you work with amateur or hobbyist fighters too?

Absolutely. Whether you're competing at state level or rolling twice a week for fitness, your rehab gets the same sport-specific approach.
